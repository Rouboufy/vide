Isolated by design

Vide never loads or rewrites the user’s unrelated system-Neovim configuration. Settings, plugins, logs, and sessions have separate XDG paths.

Platform status and limitations

Clear about what has been verified

Observed

  • Arch Linux, Ubuntu 24.04, and Debian 12 AppImage startup
  • Alacritty, tmux 3.7b, and real OpenSSH PTY paths
  • Unicode, mouse, paste, resize, and terminal restoration regression suites

Still being verified

  • macOS and WSL end-to-end workflows
  • Additional GUI terminal observations
  • Some terminals cannot distinguish every Cmd, Ctrl, Alt, and Shift sequence

Plugins that need ownership of the outer terminal UI may be incompatible. See plugin compatibility, terminal compatibility, the complete documentation, and open issues.
